First release of **Lythos MSEW** — design and analysis of mechanically stabilised earth (MSE) walls, driven from the browser. A sibling of Lythos Bearing, Lythos Settle and LythosFEA, with the same architecture and theme; English and Turkish throughout.

**External stability** — sliding (reinforced fill, foundation soil, geosynthetic interface), overturning, eccentricity; bearing capacity under the effective width by Vesić, Meyerhof, Brinch Hansen, Terzaghi and EN 1997-1 side by side.

**Internal stability, layer by layer** — AASHTO Simplified Method: Tmax per layer, tensile, pullout, connection and sliding along each layer.

**Reinforcement** — steel strips (corrosion over the design life), polymer strips, geogrids and geotextiles, and a catalogue of typical market products: HA strips 40×4 – 60×5 (S355, Grade 65), PET strips 20–100 kN, HDPE and PET geogrids, PET and PP geotextiles.

**Also** — pseudo-static earthquake, ASD or LRFD, the required reinforcement length, a layout generator, a height study with CSV / XLSX export, and PDF / HTML / Word reports.

**Run it** — download the wheel below and `pip install lythosmsew-0.1.0-py3-none-any.whl`, then `lythos-msew`; or from a clone, `pip install numpy matplotlib reportlab` and `python main.py`.

Not included: global / compound stability, settlement, drainage — check them separately.
